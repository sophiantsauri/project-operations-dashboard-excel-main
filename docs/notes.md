# Interview Notes – Project Operations Dashboard

## 1. Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

I built an Excel-based project operations dashboard that simulates how projects are tracked from setup through closeout. It includes billing tracking, inventory monitoring, document control, and workflow stages. The goal was to create a centralized system that improves visibility, coordination, and efficiency.

---

## 2. Why I Built It

To understand how operations, inventory, and project coordination work together in real-world environments like construction, data centers, and infrastructure projects.

---

## 3. Key Things to Mention

- Inventory tracking
- Material staging and usage
- Billing and invoice tracking
- Workflow coordination
- Data accuracy and reporting
- Supporting project teams

---

## 4. If They Ask “Do You Have Inventory Experience?”

Say:

Yes — I’ve worked with tracking operational resources and also built an inventory tracking system in Excel that simulates material receipt, staging, usage, and monitoring. It helped me understand how inventory directly impacts project timelines and efficiency.

---

## 5. If They Ask “Do You Know Inventory Software?”

Say:

I haven’t used specific inventory platforms yet, but I’ve built structured tracking systems and I’m very comfortable learning new tools quickly. My Excel system already follows similar logic to inventory management systems.

---

## 6. If They Ask About Forklift

Say:

I don’t currently have a forklift certification, but I’m open and willing to obtain one immediately if required.

---

## 7. Strengths to Emphasize

- Attention to detail
- Accuracy
- Organization
- Fast learner
- Data-driven thinking
- Team coordination

---

## 8. Closing Statement

I’m excited about this role because it combines operations, inventory, and coordination. I’m confident I can quickly adapt, contribute, and grow within the team.
